I'm a graphic design student at the Estonian Academy of Arts, passionate about creating meaningful visual identities and exploring the intersection of traditional and digital design.

My work focuses on brand identity systems, editorial design, and user interfaces. I believe good design should be both functional and emotionally resonant — telling stories that connect people to ideas.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring Tallinn's architecture, sketching in cafés, or experimenting with risograph printing.
