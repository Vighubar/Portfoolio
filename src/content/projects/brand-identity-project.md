## Research & Discovery

The project began with an extensive research phase — visiting the brewery, interviewing the founders, and studying the competitive landscape of Estonian craft beer brands.

Key insights from research:
- The founders draw direct inspiration from Nordic foraging traditions
- Their target audience values authenticity and local provenance
- Existing competitors lean heavily on either "hipster" aesthetics or overly traditional imagery

## Concept Development

I explored three distinct directions before arriving at the final concept:

1. **Folk Modern** — Reinterpreting Estonian folk textile patterns as geometric brand elements
2. **Botanical** — Using native plant illustrations as the primary visual language
3. **Terrain** — Abstract topographic forms inspired by Estonian landscapes

The final direction merged elements from Folk Modern and Botanical, creating a system that feels both rooted and contemporary.

## Design System

The identity system includes:
- Primary and secondary logo lockups
- A modular pattern library derived from folk motifs
- Seasonal color palettes (4 per year)
- Custom label system for 12+ beer varieties
- Environmental and merchandise applications

## Reflection

This project taught me the importance of balancing client heritage with forward-looking design. The modular approach allowed the brand to feel cohesive while remaining flexible enough for seasonal storytelling.
